This glossary provides standard definitions for core architectural concepts, licensing terms, and verification systems across Extra Scoop.

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Silicon Trust™** | A layered provenance framework that combines automated synthetic-media analysis, hardware-backed attestation, and cryptographically signed manifests to guarantee the integrity of breaking news media. |
| **Digital Deed** | An immutable ownership certificate anchored to a public layer-2 ledger and decentralised storage, establishing legal title, chain of custody, and commercial licensing terms for captured media. |
| **Trust Score** | A dynamic reputation rating computed from multi-factor verification signals, hardware attestation, and community consensus to measure creator credibility and media authenticity. |
| **Smart Escrow** | An automated settlement mechanism that locks enterprise bids in secure custody and executes instant payout upon mutually accepted licensing terms. |
| **Concord Dealroom** | An institutional negotiation interface enabling verified newsrooms to submit commercial bids, propose custom terms, and acquire exclusive media licences directly from eyewitnesses. |
| **Deed Vault** | A cryptographically verifiable repository where creators and newsrooms inspect, track, and manage all active, pending, and settled digital deeds and licences. |
| **Ephemeral Messenger** | An end-to-end encrypted communication channel engineered with post-quantum key encapsulation and deterministic memory purging to safeguard source confidentiality during licensing negotiations. |
| **Newsroom Terminal** | A real-time intelligence desk featuring configurable multi-stream columns, acoustic radar alerts, and automated wire export for broadcast editorial teams. |
| **Wire Exporter** | A formatting engine that converts verified media packages into IPTC NewsML-G2 XML and webhook payloads compatible with major broadcast CMS systems. |
| **Forensic Invariance** | A security guarantee ensuring that unwatermarked raw source assets are permanently purged from public access once protected and sealed by the provenance pipeline. |
| **Blind ID** | A cryptographically masked session identifier that shields sensitive whistleblower credentials while maintaining verifiable reputational provenance. |
| **C2PA Manifest** | A standardised digital provenance record injected directly into media containers to prove asset origin, capture parameters, and editorial integrity. |

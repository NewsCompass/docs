The Extra Scoop mobile app enables you to record breaking news footage, certify its provenance, license it to global news organisations, and get paid directly.

## Step 1: Install and Launch

1. Download Extra Scoop from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
2. Launch the application and select **Get Started**.
3. Complete the initial registration using your email address or biometric sign-in.

## Step 2: Grant Permissions

To certify that your footage is genuine and location-accurate, the app requires key device permissions:

* **Camera & Microphone:** Required for in-app video recording and cryptographic timestamping.
* **Location Services:** Required to embed verifiable geographic coordinates into your dispatch metadata.
* **Push Notifications:** Required to receive instant alerts when newsrooms bid on your scoops.

## Step 3: Configure Security & Identity

During initial setup, you establish your security baseline:

1. **Security PIN:** Set a 6-digit PIN used to authorise sensitive actions, such as bank account changes and cryptographic key rotations.
2. **Biometric Unlock:** Enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint authentication for fast, secure access.
3. **Source Privacy:** Choose whether your public profile displays your verified display name or a pseudonymous Blind ID for anonymous reporting.

## Step 4: Choose Your Reporting Persona

During onboarding, you select an initial reporting role to bootstrap your personalised news feed and alert profile:

| Reporting Role | In-App Description | Primary Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Witness** | *"I'm there. I record. I report."* | Real-time breaking developments and live on-the-scene capture. |
| **Field Reporter** | *"I provide pro-level, deep coverage."* | In-depth reporting, multi-angle documentation, and comprehensive coverage. |
| **Truth Seeker** | *"I verify facts and maintain the score."* | Fact verification, integrity evaluation, and community credibility consensus. |

### Dynamic Recommendation Adaptation

As you use Extra Scoop, the recommendation engine dynamically refines your discovery feed based on your active reporting behaviour—whether you focus on raw upload speed, deep multi-source investigation, or community verification.

You can update your persona selection and notification preferences at any time in **Settings > Preferences**.
